1. Beastmaster

A wild and untamed adventurer with a deep bond to nature. The Beastmaster commands a loyal animal companion that aids them in combat and exploration. They are adept trackers, skilled archers, and protectors of wildlife.

2. Runesmith

A master craftsman who imbues weapons, armor, and artifacts with magical runes. The Runesmith is a versatile support class, creating enchanted items and traps that turn the tide of battle. Their knowledge of ancient symbols makes them invaluable for solving puzzles and unlocking mysteries.

3. Timeweaver

A mysterious figure with the rare ability to manipulate the flow of time. The Timeweaver can speed up allies, slow down foes, or even reverse brief moments to undo mistakes. Their powers are both awe-inspiring and feared, as they seem to exist slightly out of sync with the present.

4. Stormcaller

A conduit of the skies, wielding the raw power of storms. The Stormcaller commands lightning, thunder, and fierce winds to devastate enemies and shield allies. Their connection to the weather makes them a force of nature, bringing chaos to the battlefield.
